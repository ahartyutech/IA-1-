Chapter 4: The Proper Use of Time

Time management is crucial for success. People often spend too much energy on urgent tasks rather than focusing on what truly matters. The key is to plan time around long-term goals to take control of your life.

I've realized I sometimes waste time on small tasks or distractions instead of focusing on growth-oriented activities. My solution is to improve my daily planning and prioritize tasks that align with my long-term goals.

Chapter 5: Surround Yourself With the Best People

Your success and mindset are greatly influenced by the people around you. Successful people spend time with others who are motivated and positive, as relationships can either foster growth or hold you back.

This has made me evaluate my friendships more carefully. I'm now more conscious about spending time with people who motivate and challenge me to improve, recognizing that some relationships may not align with my growth goals.

Learning extends beyond formal education. Success comes from continuous self-education through reading, observing successful people, and reflecting on experiences.

I'm actively pursuing knowledge through various channels — projects, online tutorials, and learning from inspiring individuals. This reinforces my belief that continuous learning in different forms is just as valuable as formal education.

Chapter 7: Setting Goals and Making Them Happen

Goal-setting is fundamental to personal growth. Writing down goals and breaking them into smaller steps makes them more achievable, and the journey itself contributes to personal development.

I'm now implementing a more structured approach to my goals. By writing down my personal, academic, and career objectives and breaking them into manageable steps, I'm creating a clearer path to achievement.
